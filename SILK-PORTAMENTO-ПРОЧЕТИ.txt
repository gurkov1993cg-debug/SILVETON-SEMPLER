WHAT CHANGED
- SILVETON portamento: musical eased semitone glide, with smooth entrance
  and precise arrival at the set TIME. No trailing post-time filter drift.
- Close intervals receive the softest easing; intermediate-legato switches
  preserve pitch and bounded momentum to avoid an audible corner.
- BALANCE (3..40) now controls glide softness instead of adding milliseconds
  of pitch lag. The UI does not label BALANCE as milliseconds anymore.
- TIME / TIME СЪСЕДНИ and their numeric ranges are unchanged.
- KRONOS intentionally preserves its existing linear glide/time/rate contract.
- New native regression test, and both workflow checks updated.
